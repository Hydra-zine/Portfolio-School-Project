Intro C++ course covering variables, control flow, functions, arrays, and OOP basics.

Projects

  • Tic-Tac-Toe — two-player console game with 2D array board

MAT 2020 — Calculus II

Integration techniques, sequences and series, Taylor polynomials, polar coordinates, complex numbers.

Topics

  • Integration by parts, trig substitution, partial fractions
  • Convergence tests: ratio, root, comparison, integral
  • Taylor and Maclaurin series
  • Parametric curves and polar coordinates
  • Complex numbers and Euler's formula

First-year writing. Focus on argument, evidence, and revision, using techniques such as ethos, pathos and logos.

Assignments

  • Portfolio Reflection Essay — synthesis of growth across four course learning outcomes

  • Research Essay — Manhattan Project scientists' moral opposition to nuclear weapons (Chicago style)
